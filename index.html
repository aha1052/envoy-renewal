1. 리뉴얼 배경 및 목적

현행 사이트의 주요 문제점

  • 국가별 메뉴 구조로 투어 상품 탐색이 어려움
  • 상품 상세 페이지 구성이 일관되지 않아 가격, 일정, 포함 사항 등 핵심 정보 확인이 어려움
  • 가격이 상품 소개 페이지상에서는 GEL/AMD로만 표기되어 해외 고객의 비교·판단이 어려움
  • 2016년 이후 갱신되지 않은 페이지가 다수 존재함
  • 예약 요청 양식의 입력 항목(12개)이 많아 문의 전환에 불리함

리뉴얼 목표

목표주요 실행 방안
상품 탐색성 개선투어 유형별 메뉴와 All Tours 필터(Day / Multi-day, Group / Private, 국가) 병행, 메인 화면에 대표 상품 및 추천 투어 배치
상세 페이지 표준화공통 항목과 상품별 선택 항목으로 구성된 템플릿 적용, 첫 화면에서 가격·운영 정보·핵심 포함 사항 제공
문의·예약 전환율 제고목적별 버튼 구분(상단 Plan Your Trip, 상세 Request a Booking), WhatsApp 보조 배치, 문의 양식 간소화
고객 신뢰도 제고실제 고객 후기(출처·작성 시기 표기)와 플랫폼 평점, 국가별 현지 운영 이력, Why Travel with Envoy 섹션 운영
브랜드 가치 전달기존 Donation 페이지를 Our Impact로 개편, Envoy Mission 실제 사례 소개
계열 서비스 연계Envoy Hostel을 투어의 자산(구 트빌리시 출발 거점, 건물 내 투어 사무실)으로 소개, 루프탑 Cafe 연계 동선 구성
모바일 우선 설계모바일 첫 화면에 상품 배너·문의 버튼 배치, 투어 페이지 롱스크롤 구성

  • 사이트 구성: 투어 사이트와 호스텔 사이트를 별도 운영하되 Envoy 단일 브랜드로 통일하고, About Envoy·Envoy Way·Our Impact 콘텐츠는 공유

1. Background and Objectives

Key Issues with the Current Site

  • Country-based menu structure makes tour products difficult to find
  • Inconsistent tour detail pages make key information such as price, itinerary and inclusions difficult to confirm
  • Prices on tour pages are shown only in GEL/AMD, making comparison difficult for international customers
  • Many pages have not been updated since 2016
  • The booking request form has too many fields (12), which discourages inquiries

Renewal Objectives

  • Site role: tour discovery and inquiry generation (Discover and Inquire)
  • Site configuration: tours and hostel sites operated separately under a single Envoy brand, sharing About Envoy, Envoy Way and Our Impact content
  • Booking system: introduction of Bókun (instant booking for day tours, request-based booking for multi-day journeys and group products); see Section 8 (TBD)
  • Site language: English (TBD)

Customer Review Guidelines

  • Only reviews from customers who actually took part in a tour are published; quoted platform reviews show the source, date and a link to the original

  • Sources: existing TripAdvisor and Google reviews, post-tour review request emails, customer photos and videos published with consent
  • Reviews are categorised by tour and managed in the CMS; tour cards show the rating and review count

  • Common items apply to all products and optional items according to product characteristics, so that day tour pages do not become excessively long
  • Price, tour details and a summary of key inclusions are visible on the first screen
  • Large photos per itinerary day instead of a thumbnail gallery

Common Items

  1. Main photo, tour name, one-line introduction, tags
  2. Price: group/private prices and price basis (per person or per vehicle/group), Request a Booking (primary button), WhatsApp (secondary)
  3. Tour details: duration, operating days or season, departure point, group size, guide language, difficulty
  4. Summary of key inclusions
  5. Highlights (4–6)
  6. Itinerary (by day for multi-day products, by time for day tours)
  7. Inclusions and exclusions
  8. Departure dates or operating days and requests for other dates (group products use a preferred-dates inquiry instead of a departure table)
  9. Booking conditions: booking and cancellation terms, FAQ
  10. Customer reviews: rating and review count for the tour, real customer reviews (with source and date)
  11. Related tours

Optional Items (per product)

  • Accommodation (multi-day products)
  • Route map
  • Guide and operations team
  • Our Impact: social contribution of the journey
  • Stay with Envoy: stays at Envoy Hostel before and after the tour, the tour office and the rooftop cafe Common Ground (multi-day products, cross-border tours)
  • Travel preparation: what to bring, church dress code, passports, weather

Other Standards

  • Price and Request a Booking button fixed at the bottom of the mobile screen
  • Reply time, cancellation summary and tour rating placed next to the booking button
  • Departure dates, prices and departure status (available, filling fast, sold out) edited directly in the CMS
  • The two lead products are adapted into English from their existing product pages and applied to this template

Day Tour Standards

  • Tour details: duration, operating days, departure point and time, group size, guide language
  • Itinerary: a single time-based block (no expandable day-by-day sections)
  • Price: group (per person) and private (per vehicle) prices shown in a table with operating days
  • Optional items for accommodation, guide introduction and Stay with Envoy are not applied

Booking Flow

  1. Request: customer selects product, date, number of travellers and group/private, then submits the request form (reduced from 12 fields to about 7); confirmation email to the customer and alert to staff
  2. Availability check: guide and vehicle assignment confirmed; alternative dates proposed if full
  3. Payment: online payment at booking for day tours; payment link sent after confirmation for multi-day journeys and group products (payment method TBD)
  4. Booking confirmed: voucher and meeting point information
  5. Pre-departure information: pickup, weather, what to bring (email and WhatsApp)
  6. After the tour: review request, additional tours and the rooftop cafe Common Ground

Booking System

  • Current: requests received through an on-site booking request form (Tours Booking Request Form) and checked individually by staff without online payment; no dedicated booking system in use
  • Proposal: introduction of Bókun, a booking system built for tours. It maintains continuity with the current request-based process while allowing different booking methods by product type

Build-time Decisions

  • URL structure: lowercase English with hyphens (/tours/[product-name], /destinations/[country], /about/[page])

1. 리뉴얼 배경 및 목적

현행 사이트의 주요 문제점

  • 예레반·트빌리시 지점을 같은 단계에 둔 지점별 메뉴 구조로, 트빌리시 단일 운영 체제와 맞지 않음
  • 공식 사이트 방문객을 직접 예약으로 유도하는 장치(예약 버튼, 직접 예약 혜택)가 없음
  • 사진과 디자인이 오래되어 부티크 호스텔의 분위기와 여행자 교류의 활기가 전달되지 않음
  • 후기, 소식, Instagram 등 갱신 콘텐츠가 드러나지 않아 운영 중인 사이트라는 인상이 약함
  • 운영 이력, 평점, 수상 이력 등 신뢰 요소가 페이지 하단에 배치되어 있음
  • 투어 연계가 메뉴 링크 수준에 그침
  • Donation이 단순 기부 메뉴로 운영되어 브랜드 가치가 전달되지 않음

Key Issues with the Current Site

  • Branch-based menu structure placing Yerevan and Tbilisi at the same level, which no longer matches the single Tbilisi operation
